Vichar Media

SPOTLIGHT

Independent investigative journalism · No access journalism

What is it?

Vichar Media is an independent investigative journalism company, launching June 2026. Built on one principle: questions don't need permission. No sponsored stories. No access journalism. Just evidence, interviews, and accountability. Building it from the ground up — and that's the exciting part.

"We don't chase stories. We follow questions until the answers can't hide anymore."
▶ YouTube 📷 Instagram in LinkedIn

Questions I'm Currently Chasing

  • ❓ How do you build a newsroom that never has to compromise on independence?
  • ❓ What happens when the stories nobody wants published are the ones the public needs most?
  • ❓ Can independent media in India actually sustain itself without selling its soul?
